# Menu bar panel UI

## Summary

The menu bar item now opens a custom SwiftUI panel (`MenuBarExtra` with `.window` style) in place of the plain menu. The panel has:
- a status header
- mode tiles
- a timer card with a live countdown ring
- an agenda of upcoming events
- collapsible settings

## Why

The request was for a more interactive panel that's easier to use than the native menu. A native menu can't show a countdown, a progress ring or calendar colours, and choosing a mode needed a submenu.

## Design

- **Header:** an icon tinted by mode (green Calendar, orange Always, indigo Timer, grey Off/idle) that pulses while awake for an event or timer. Under it, a title, a short explanation and the next scheduled wake.
- **Mode tiles:** four equal tiles. Clicking Timer opens duration chips and only starts the timer once you pick one, so nothing changes by accident.
- **Timer card:** `TimelineView(.periodic(by: 1))` with a ring showing time left over total time, plus +30m, +1h and Stop.
- **Agenda:** `controller.agenda`, up to 4 ongoing or upcoming events in the next 7 days. Each shows a calendar colour bar, a day and time range, and a "Now" badge while it's keeping the Mac awake. It's dimmed in Always and Off modes, where the calendar is ignored.
- **Settings:** collapsible, and the open/closed state is saved with `@AppStorage`. Switches line up on the right and the whole row is clickable. Calendars are checkboxes with colour dots in a scrolling list.
- **Footer:** Launch at Login, a ⋯ menu (Remove Wake Helper…), Refresh (⌘R) and Quit (⌘Q).

**Toolchain constraint:** with the Command Line Tools, `@State` fails to build (`SwiftUIMacros.StateMacro` plugin not found). Local view state uses `@StateObject` with a tiny `Flag` class instead. AGENT.md records this.

**Debug preview:** `--preview-panel` opens the same panel in a normal window. `--snapshot <png>` saves it as an image with `cacheDisplay`, which needs no Screen Recording permission. `AwakeController.shared` lets the panel and the preview window share one controller.

**Options considered and not picked**
- *Keeping the native menu and adding a submenu:* no live countdown or colours, and harder to use.
- *A separate settings window:* more pieces to maintain. A collapsible section keeps everything in one place.

- `README.md`: Modes and Panel sections, and a development note.

## Verified / not verified

- ✅ Builds and runs.
- ✅ Snapshots checked in Always mode (including the calendar-access card) and Timer mode (countdown, agenda with real events, expanded settings), in dark appearance.
- ❌ Not yet tested: light appearance, clicking through every control in the real menu bar panel, and hover and press effects. The snapshots are static, and the offscreen render greys out switch accent colours.
